Why it matters

Audit isn't optional in Malta

Which is exactly why it should be painless. Here's what's at stake — and how we take it off your plate.

It's the law in Malta

Unlike many countries, every Maltese company — regardless of size — must file audited financial statements each year.

Deadlines that bite

Late filing at the MBR triggers penalties that grow over time. We keep you ahead of every deadline.

More than a signature

A good audit gives banks, investors and your board confidence in your numbers — and surfaces issues early.
